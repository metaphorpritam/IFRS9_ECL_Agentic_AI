# SICR staging report -- relative lifetime-PD test

Generated by `analysis/staging_exhibits.py` (engine/staging.py; notes section 2.2).

## Rule under test

* Stage 2 iff lifetime_pd_now > 2.0x lifetime_pd_orig (relative, same remaining-life window) AND annualised add-on > 0.5pp p.a. (ann = 1 - (1 - PD_life)^(4/R)).
* Probation: 2 consecutive trigger-free quarters before Stage 2 -> 1 (window-equivalent form).
* **30-DPD backstop: STRUCTURALLY INERT on this dataset.** The DCR panel has no delinquency ladder (status is only performing / default / payoff), so the implemented hook (`dpd_col='dpd_time'`) can never fire; Stage-2 below is quantitative-trigger-only and would be strictly larger with a live 30-DPD backstop.

Reading: in the calm quarter the relative test stages (almost) nobody at any threshold -- deterioration since origination simply has not happened -- while in the stress quarter the doubling convention (2x) moves roughly three quarters of the live book to lifetime ECL, and the choice between 2x and 4x swings the Stage-2 population by tens of percentage points of the book. The threshold is the single loudest governance dial in the impairment estimate (notes section 2.2 pitfall).

## Documented simplifications

* Frozen-covariate lifetime PDs on BOTH legs (pd_term_structure rung-1 convention): no macro path, no LTV amortisation; scenario conditioning arrives with the satellite-model rung.
* 30-DPD backstop inert (no delinquency ladder in DCR) -- loud note above.
* Origination macro for pre-window vintages clamps to the earliest observed quarter (flagged per loan, `orig_macro_approx`); origination market rate is the per-quarter MEDIAN of loan-matched rate_time; zero-coded origination note rates fall back to the current note rate (`orig_rate_proxy`).
* No low-credit-risk exemption, no qualitative/watchlist triggers (fields do not exist in the dataset).
* Loans at/past scheduled maturity get a floored one-quarter window; payoff-quarter rows are excluded from the staged book as derecognitions.
* Models fitted on the full training window; the t=20 snapshot is an in-sample illustration, not a walk-forward simulation.
