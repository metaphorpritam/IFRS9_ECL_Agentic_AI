Widget demo

Vasicek one-factor PIT-from-TTC conditioning — every widget primitive exercised on the real formula

This page exists to exercise notes/assets/js/widgets.js end to end (makeSlider, makeNumberInput, makeLinePlot, makeLiveTable) and to double as the reference example a chapter author copies. The formula is the Vasicek one-factor conditioning of a through-the-cycle PD onto a systematic factor $Z$ (see tests/fixtures/compute_vasicek.py, the golden source):

$$ PD_{PIT}(Z) \;=\; \Phi\!\left( \frac{\Phi^{-1}(PD_{TTC}) - \sqrt{\rho}\, Z}{\sqrt{1-\rho}} \right) $$

where $\Phi$ is the standard-normal CDF, $PD_{TTC}$ the through-the-cycle (unconditional) PD, $\rho$ the asset correlation, and $Z \sim N(0,1)$ the systematic credit-cycle factor (negative $Z$ = downturn → higher $PD_{PIT}$).

At the defaults ($PD_{TTC}=2\%$, $\rho=0.12$) the table below reproduces the golden values in tests/fixtures/compute_vasicek.py exactly (e.g. $PD_{PIT}(Z=-2.0) = 7.34\%$, $PD_{PIT}(Z=+2.0)=0.17\%$) — this page's JS normal-CDF/PPF are independent re-implementations of the same closed-form used by the Python fixture (which uses scipy.stats.norm), so agreement to 2 displayed decimals cross-checks both.
